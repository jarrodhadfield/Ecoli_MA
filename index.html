Ecoli_MA

Ecoli_MA contains scripts used for mapping reads and calling variants in sequenced mutation accumulation lines of Escherichia coli. These data were used in Grosse-Sommer, Newman and Hadfield (2026) to quantify the fitness effects of new mutations and in Grosse-Sommer and Hadfield (2026) as part of an analysis of E. coli mutational patterns. The main script for mapping reads, calling variants and testing the accuracy of the pipeline is Mutation_Calling, and can be found in scripts/.

References:

Grosse-Sommer and Hadfield (2026) Selection bias in microbial mutation accumulation studies and the impact of colony growth. bioRxiv 2026.05.28.725302

Grosse-Sommer, Newman and Hadfield (2026) Fitness effects of new mutations are small and heavily confounded with non-genetic sources of variation in Escherichia coli. bioRxiv 2026.05.28.725302
